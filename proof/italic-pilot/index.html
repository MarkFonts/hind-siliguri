Class map: the Bangla anchor choice

Full-height verticals and the headline are stems and straights, sheared at θ; loops and bowls are anchored rounds at plain shear; only closed free rounds (o O 0 ০) lean back.

Evidence for two class decisions

The rigid spine (I16) on a Bangla inflected stroke (১, Light): on, then off. On, the top of the stroke thins to a needle and fails the gate; off, it passes.

Entry cuts keep the roman angle (I15): on a sans the upright terminal cut leaves a wedge on a and 3, and a stem-end wedge at the B's top left (Bold).
